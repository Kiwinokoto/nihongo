# Little Memories — original illustrated story journal

**Scope:** An optional gallery reached through **My journal → Little Memories**.
The home hero, its SVG carousel, map, exercises, other dialogs and progress
keys are unchanged.

## Source image inventory and five story moments

The owner supplied **four individual high-detail illustrations** (two
panoramas, plus the upper and lower halves of a paired panorama), as well as
two separate *whole-page UI concepts*. The latter guide styling, but are not
standalone story art and should not be committed as in-site screenshots.

The five **story moments** below deliberately include one **composite
view** made by joining the existing upper and lower source panels. It is not
a claim of five separately generated illustrations.

| Memory | Visual | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I · The First Steps | `assets/memories/first-steps.webp` — wide village panorama with travellers at a distance | One saved successful learning result |
| II · The Bridge in Bloom | `assets/memories/blossom-bridge.webp` — upper half of previously supplied two-panel village/train art | All 46 basic hiragana |
| III · The Next Stop | `assets/memories/next-stop.webp` — lower half, mountain train and platform | All 46 basic katakana |
| IV · A Closer View | `assets/memories/closer-view.webp` — close-up of the adult travellers | Eight distinct correctly recognised advanced kana readings |
| V · Two Paths, One Journey | **Same bridge and station images stacked vertically in-browser** as a reunited diptych; no additional duplicate media | All 46 hiragana **and** all 46 katakana |

The four optimized RGB WebPs come only from the owner's earlier AI-generated
concepts, not from scraping manga/media. The top village frame is cropped from
rows 0–377 of the 1983 × 793 paired original. The railway panel is cropped
from that same original's lower rows. Maximum display width 1920 px, WebP
quality 86. Each image is below 600 KB. The originals remain outside the
public repository; only optimized gallery assets are committed.

## Unlock, privacy, performance, usability

- The pure `assets/memories-data.js` maps **existing** achievements (from
  `assets/story-milestones.js`) to scenes; two-key achievements use logical
  AND. There is **no new persisted state**, profile or story-view history.
- Locked cards never construct an `img` URL. The final diptych has neither
  component loaded until *both* collections are complete. Once unlocked,
  the same locally hosted assets can be reused by the browser cache.
- The gallery stays a separate optional dialog in soft rose/sakura styling.
  Do not move detailed art onto the learning map or replace the calm hero.
- Escape, sticky close, return-to-journal, keyboard browsing, and mobile
  dialog width remain usable; system reduced-motion preferences honoured.
- Japanese narration is editorial atmosphere, not a graded language lesson.
  Human Japanese text review and real Safari/Mac/mobile QA remain future work.
