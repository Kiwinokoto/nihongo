# Kotoba no Tabi 🌸 · ことばの旅

A gentle little journey through Japanese reading, sounds, and handwriting.
Public and dependency-free at runtime.

**Live:** https://nihongo.kiwinokoto.fr

## Garden of hiragana

- A small storybook map, with one playable destination (the hiragana garden)
- All **46 basic hiragana** in **10 progressively unlocked rows**; previous rows stay accessible
- Animated brush-stroke order from AnimCJK, including replay
- Mouse, touch and stylus drawing with a faint guide, undo and clear
- A recognition quiz: correct answers collect characters and unlock the next row
- A small travel journal, spaced reviews (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days) and gentle retry scheduling
- Optional Japanese speech via **device-installed browser voices**; the app explicitly reports when none is available
- Preserves the progress saved by the first V1 prototype, under the same localStorage key
- **Katakana station:** the 46 basic katakana in ten rows, animated brush guides, mouse/touch tracing, recognition quizzes, spaced reviews and a **separate** browser save.
- **Starting village:** independent illustrated welcome and links to the other playable stops. A Japanese-voice availability check and optional Mac installation instructions do **not** record the user's voice or access the microphone.
- **Sound fallback:** for the first five vowels (both scripts), we bundle small public-domain human recordings that work without any Japanese device TTS voice. The remaining sounds and phrases still require a Japanese native system voice (see `docs/AUDIO_SOURCES.md`).
- **Comfort:** desktop backdrop clicks close dialogs, with a sticky X for long content; touch/pen users avoid accidental outside dismissals.
- **Home:** the banner is a quiet welcome, and the map is the only main destination picker (no redundant instant-Hiragana button). Original village art introduces two travellers. Find small story hints among the Hiragana word postcards.

- Optional little word postcards from the kana collection: recognise real words made of kana you have already collected, with separate browser-local progress (does not affect kana unlocks)
- A very small **optional Lantern Evening** mini-story appears after the first collected word postcard. Three original illustrated festival scenes and short Japanese lines with translations, no forced participation and no story-tracking. The main map remains unchanged.
- **The little food lane** (the former locked tea-house stop) is now available directly from the map. Explore grilled fish, yakitori, octopus bites, nattō, okonomiyaki and grilled eel, in an original yatai scene. Listen (when a Japanese device voice exists) and practise polite ordering with a four-choice game. Completed orders use a separate browser key and never change kana, word-postcard or story progress.

**Crucial distinction:** The animation shows the reference stroke order, and drawing
counts self-reported *practice*. It is **not handwriting recognition**. The app does
not claim to judge the geometry, stroke direction, stroke order or correctness of a
drawn character. Mastery is awarded only by a recognition exercise. This is deliberate
until a carefully evaluated handwriting matcher exists.

## Run and test

No bundler, package install, database or server application required.

```bash
node --check assets/app.js
node --test tests/*.test.mjs
python3 tools/draw_landscape.py
python3 -m http.server 8787
```

Open http://localhost:8787/. Optional browser E2E checks can be run locally with
Chromium/Playwright; no Playwright or CI runner is required to serve the site.

## Security, privacy and deployment

Stable page and asset URLs use `Cache-Control: no-cache, must-revalidate` so browsers can revalidate after a release. This does not clear local learner progress.

The static site is served in an isolated Nginx container on the personal VPS
through the existing Traefik `traefik_network` and Let's Encrypt resolver.
It collects no student profiles, has no database or analytics, and contains no
secrets. Ordinary web server / reverse-proxy access logs may record IP addresses.

Progress is **only in local browser storage**, not backed up or synced between devices.
Google Fonts are requested from Google by a visitor's browser. Do not commit
credentials, visitor progress, or private files.

Before deployment check canonical GitHub main, runtime commit, local state, tests,
AgentCtl `repo:Kiwinokoto/nihongo` and deployment/service/Docker leases. Never
force-push or change other applications. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and
[BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md) for coordination and next work. The future optional
romance-easter-egg direction (not implemented) is noted in
[docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md](docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md).


### Little Memories — illustrated journal

Open **My journal → Little Memories** for a separate, pastel-sakura
illustrated gallery using three high-detail original concept panoramas
previously supplied by the project owner. The original main map/hero and
all learning activities are unchanged. The distant village is unlocked
after one successful recognition/order; a closer view of the two travellers
after eight advanced readings; a train image after completing all 46 basic
katakana. Locked art is not downloaded until earned; the gallery records
no views or personal preferences. See `docs/LITTLE_MEMORIES.md`.

### Story scenes and milestones

The quiet village hero now has an optional, manual story carousel. One
**correctly completed** recognition/ordering result from any activity
unlocks a quick railway teaser; after eight distinct advanced readings the
existing close-up of the two travellers is available there too.
Arrow buttons, keyboard and touch swipe navigate earned scenes; no autoplay.
The travel journal celebrates separate 46-hiragana and 46-katakana milestones
and marks Kanji Shrine as future. No new learner profile, story history or
extra save key is needed: earlier achievements are recognised immediately.
See `docs/STORY_MILESTONES.md`.

The five locally hosted public-domain vowel recordings are now **one sound
per click**: the original source audio repeated each vowel three times,
so the first complete utterance is retained. A reviewed 41-clip extension
is planned but Wikimedia rate limits prevented this bulk import;
the remaining basic kana still need a Japanese browser voice.
See `docs/AUDIO_SOURCES.md`.

### Beyond the first kana

The Hiragana Garden and Katakana Station both include an optional
**Beyond the basics ✧** workshop. It has 70 reading cards for hiragana and
94 for katakana, grouped into voiced kana (dakuten/handakuten),
contracted syllables (yōon), small kana, long vowels and consonant pauses;
the katakana side adds selected modern loanword combinations.
The workshop tracks its own reading discoveries under
`kotoba-no-tabi.advanced-kana.v1`. It never awards basic-kana mastery.

After eight distinct advanced readings, an original **close-up postcard**
featuring the same two travellers may be opened from the workshop. Their
earlier distant village scene remains on the homepage. No story viewing,
romance preference or identity is recorded.

See `docs/ADVANCED_KANA.md` for scope and language-review limitations.

### How complete are the kana?

The two interactive lessons cover **46 basic hiragana** and **46 basic katakana**,
with writing guides, recognition and separate practice progress. These are
the foundational signs, not every Japanese sound combination. Voiced variants (`が`/`ガ`, `ぱ`/`パ`), small kana, contracted
`きゃ`/`キャ`, long vowels, consonant pauses and selected loanword
variants are now **introduced as reading patterns** in the optional
advanced workshop, not integrated into stroke-tracing or automatic
handwriting verification. Uncommon/historical and additional loanword
spellings, fluent listening and contextual vocabulary remain future work.

Drawing remains **ungraded practice**. The optional recogniser feasibility
study is documented in `docs/HANDWRITING_POC.md` and is not shipped.
